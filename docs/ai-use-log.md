## AI Use Log

This log discloses AI assistance used during database planning, implementation support, and documentation. Team members remain responsible for reviewing and validating all generated suggestions.

| Tool | Where Used | What it was used for | What the team changed / checked |
|---|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT | Database Design Planning and Documentation | Assisted in organizing the database design, identifying entities and their attributes, defining primary and foreign keys, and documenting relationships and cardinalities. It also helped structure the relational schema, data dictionary, ER diagram content, screen-to-data map, and Room implementation plan. | The team reviewed the proposed design against the actual Kotlin Entity and DAO files, checked table and field names, key types, relationships, and delete actions, and identified items requiring final verification. |
| Gemini | Spread and Wear Tracker Screens; WearLogEntity and WearLogDao; Database Documentation | Assisted in structuring the Spread and Wear Tracker screen states, drafting Room entity and DAO-related documentation, and identifying database information needed to support wear-tracking features. | The team reviewed the suggested queries and documentation against the WearLogEntity and WearLogDao implementation and checked the relevant field names and data types. |
| ChatGPT | Room Entity Implementation and Troubleshooting | Assisted with understanding Room annotations, composite primary keys, foreign key relationships, indexes, and auto-generated primary keys for entities such as ClothingItemTagEntity and OutfitEntity. It also provided guidance on troubleshooting a UserDao.kt syntax issue and Gradle dependencies. | The implementation was reviewed and corrected in Android Studio. The team checked the Room annotations and database relationships. The development record documents a successful build and GitHub PR #15. |
| Claude | User Info, Closet, and Wishlist Screens; WishlistItemEntity and WishlistDao | Assisted in structuring Compose screens and UI states and providing guidance on the WishlistItemEntity and WishlistDao implementation and their database requirements. | The team reviewed the suggested implementation against the actual Kotlin files, particularly the wishlist primary key, nullable itemId, and its relationship with ClothingItemEntity. |
